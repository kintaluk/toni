What is TONI?

TONI helps you choose a film for tonight. Type, tap the suggested answers, or try a voice conversation. When you’re ready, tap Find my results to build your shortlist.

Your preferences guide the recommendations. TONI searches for review evidence and checks streaming availability in your country, then explains the fit and where the evidence is limited or mixed. Availability can change, so confirm it with the provider before watching.

Recent films are the starting point. Use the quick refinements above your results to adjust runtime, mood or release year, including Any year for older films.

Voice uses Gemini Live and is an experimental beta. Response timing and audio reliability are still being refined. You can always type or use the buttons.
