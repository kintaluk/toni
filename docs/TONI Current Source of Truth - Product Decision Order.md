2. Explicitly superseded clauses

Persistent learning

Older scope: persistent viewing-history learning and long-term taste learning were outside the hackathon requirement.

Current decision: Task 04 promotes a bounded, lightweight progressive memory model into MVP. Persist enduring taste signals, service access, recommendations, confirmed viewing signals where known, likes, dislikes, rejections and corrections. Do not treat full chat history as the memory model.

Conversational experience

Older scope: full conversational recommendation was outside the hackathon requirement.

Current decision: conversational hybrid intake and bounded free-text refinement are in MVP. An unrestricted general-purpose chatbot is still out of scope.

Recommendation count

Older scope/Task 03 language: final three recommendations.

Current decision: show Top 3 for tonight first, then Show my full recommendations to reveal seven total ranked eligible recommendations where enough candidates exist. The named roles remain Best fit, Strong alternative and Worth a stretch for the first three.

Availability across expanded results

Current consequence: Task 03 availability eligibility applies to all seven exposed recommendations, not only the initial three.

Service defaults

Older ideal-experience wording allowed country-informed suggested service defaults.

Current decision: country can determine which services are shown, but no service is preselected. The user explicitly confirms access. Free/popular services cannot be assumed.

Group viewing

Older ideal experience allowed a lightweight co-viewing path.

Current decision: group viewing is out of the hackathon MVP and moved to future development.

Account/profile

Older scope treated persistent profile/learning as non-required.

Current decision: first use remains account-free, but the MVP product direction includes recommend first, save afterwards, plus lightweight returning-user memory and editable My taste controls. Build this after the anonymous end-to-end loop works.

Rubric

Older repo/Brief 1 contract: seven fixed dimensions.

Current product contract: six Film Profile dimensions: Story and writing; Pacing and structure; Performances; Tone and emotional character; Craft and execution; Accessibility and demandingness. Critical consensus/divergence is Evidence State, not a Film Profile dimension. Rewatch value is removed.

3. Decisions that remain fully in force

Core promise: Personal fit + less effort.

Film-first hackathon scope.

Primary audience: film-aware, time-poor viewers.

Secondary audience: choice-overloaded mainstream streamers.

TONI narrows choice rather than showing everything.

Evidence-led recommendations with natural-language reasoning.

Spoiler-free by default.

Decisive first, refinement second.

Live Parallel Search at runtime for the partner-track build.

Live UK/US availability as a hard eligibility constraint.

Unverified availability is not treated as available.

Raw Personal Fit Score is not shown by default.

TONI remains warm, film-aware, confident, reasoned and not over-chatty.
